## Research Methodology

### Literature Search Strategy

1. **Terminology search**: Search for exact phrases ("Temporal State Reconstruction", "bidirectional state reconstruction", "reconstruction sufficiency", etc.) across academic databases
2. **Concept search**: Search for mathematical equivalents regardless of terminology
3. **Citation tracing**: For key papers, trace backward to foundational sources and forward to current research
4. **Cross-field mapping**: Identify when the same mathematical formalism appears under different names in different fields

### Sources

- arXiv (primary for frontier work)
- Europe PMC / PubMed (for cross-disciplinary)
- OpenAlex (for bibliometrics and citation mapping)
- Web search (for recent developments, conference proceedings)
- Textbook-level references for foundational material

### Evidence Classification

Every claim labeled per the directive:
- **[A]** Experimentally established
- **[B]** Strong theoretical/mathematical result  
- **[C]** Active research / unresolved
- **[D]** Interpretation/philosophy
- **[E]** TSR original hypothesis
- **[F]** Speculation
