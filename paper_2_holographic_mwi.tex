The assumptions of Eternal Inflation and the Everettian Many-Worlds Interpretation (MWI) assert that the universe continuously generates infinite, physically realized macroscopic branches. In this paper, we demonstrate that the assumption of an infinitely instantiating multiverse is the cosmological equivalent of the Rayleigh-Jeans Ultraviolet Catastrophe. Physical macroscopic reality is quantized by the thermodynamic cost of decoherence, naturally neutralizing the infinities of the Measure Problem.

\section{I. Introduction: The Cosmological Ultraviolet Catastrophe}

The history of theoretical physics is punctuated by mathematical infinities that signal the breakdown of classical, continuous assumptions. In the early 20th century, the Rayleigh-Jeans law modeled black-body radiation by assuming energy could be divided infinitely. This continuous assumption famously led to the Ultraviolet Catastrophe---the prediction of infinite high-frequency energy. Max Planck resolved this by introducing discrete quantization ($E = h\nu$). 

Shortly thereafter, classical electromagnetism predicted that an electron orbiting a nucleus would continuously radiate energy and undergo instant orbital collapse. Niels Bohr resolved this by quantizing angular momentum ($L = mvr = n\hbar$), establishing that the universe does not render continuous physical motion between states, but rather transitions via discrete leaps.

Modern cosmology faces an identical crisis. Eternal Inflation and the Everettian Many-Worlds Interpretation assume that the macroscopic universe is continuously and infinitely divisible, generating infinite pocket universes and parallel branches. This continuous assumption results in the Measure Problem: a mathematical divergence where infinite volumes yield incalculable probabilities. 

We propose that the Measure Problem is the modern Ultraviolet Catastrophe. Just as Planck and Bohr saved physics by quantizing energy and momentum, we must quantize the physical instantiation of macroscopic reality using strict thermodynamic and holographic limits.

\section{II. The Holographic Memory Bound}

The foundational flaw of the infinite physical multiverse is its violation of fundamental informational bounds. In a de Sitter vacuum, the maximum informational capacity within a cosmological horizon is strictly limited by the Gibbons-Hawking entropy, generalized by the Bousso Covariant Entropy Bound:
\begin{equation}
S_{GH} = \frac{k_B A}{4 \ell_p^2} = \frac{3\pi k_B}{G \Lambda}
\end{equation}
where $A$ is the area of the cosmological event horizon, $\ell_p$ is the Planck length, and $\Lambda$ is the cosmological constant. 

This bounds the total number of quantum degrees of freedom accessible to any local observer. If every Everettian branch triggered by continuous quantum decoherence were fully physically instantiated, the required degrees of freedom would exponentially diverge as $N(t) \propto e^{\Gamma t}$, vastly exceeding $S_{GH}$. Under the Holographic Principle, the universe physically lacks the informational capacity (the ``memory'') to run a continuous, infinite fractal multiverse. Therefore, the infinite multiverse is a classical over-extrapolation.

\section{III. Quiescent Vacua and the Causal Patch}

To reconcile quantum branching with the Gibbons-Hawking bound, we adopt the Causal Patch Measure. In computational terms, a ``Just-In-Time'' (JIT) rendering engine only processes the geometry within a localized camera frustum. In cosmology, this corresponds to the Causal Diamond of an observer.

As demonstrated by Boddy, Carroll, and Pollack (2014), an empty de Sitter vacuum lacking an out-of-equilibrium recording device (an observer) settles into a stationary, quiescent state, satisfying the Wheeler-DeWitt equation:
\begin{equation}
\hat{H} |\Psi\rangle = 0
\end{equation}
Without an observer to force dynamical decoherence, the vacuum stops computing dynamical fluctuations. 

Applying this to MWI: when the universal wavefunction branches, the universe does not fully instantiate the parallel worlds. The unobserved branches remain as coarse-grained, uncomputed probability space. They exist as mathematical potential (Tegmark Level IV) but require zero thermodynamic capacity. The exponential infinities of eternal inflation are mathematically traced out, elegantly eliminating the Youngness and Boltzmann Brain paradoxes.

If unobserved branches remain unrendered, what triggers the physical instantiation of a specific branch? We argue that physical macroscopic reality is locked in by a localized thermodynamic toll.

\begin{equation}

\end{equation}

Reheating is the macroscopic Landauer heat cost generated by the universe erasing the unrendered background and instantiating our specific Causal Patch.
