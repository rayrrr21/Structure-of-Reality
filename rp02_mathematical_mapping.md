# Research Program 02: Mathematical Mapping
## Translating the Fisher Information Singularity to Quantum OTOCs

### 1. The Classical Divergence (Review)
In Program 01, we established that the classical Fisher Information Matrix (FIM) for a retrodicted state $x(t_0)$ diverges as the temporal gap $\Delta T$ grows. 
The Fisher Information scaled inversely with the exponent of the maximal Lyapunov exponent $\lambda_{\max}$:
\[ \mathcal{I}_{total} \propto e^{-\lambda_{\max} \Delta T} \]
When $\Delta T \approx 1/\lambda_{\max}$, the information decays to zero, and the error variance $\Sigma \succeq \mathcal{I}^{-1}$ diverges to infinity.

### 2. The Quantum Analogue: The OTOC
In quantum mechanics, the sensitivity to initial conditions is measured not by trajectories, but by the non-commutativity of operators separated by time. The **Out-of-Time-Order Correlator (OTOC)** is defined as:
\[ C(t) = - \langle [W(t), V(0)]^2 \rangle_{\beta} \]
where $W(t)$ and $V(0)$ are local operators, and $\langle \dots \rangle_{\beta}$ is the thermal expectation value at inverse temperature $\beta = 1/k_B T$.

In a quantum chaotic system, $C(t)$ exhibits exponential growth before saturating at the scrambling time $t_*$:
\[ C(t) \propto \hbar^2 e^{\lambda_Q t} \]
where $\lambda_Q$ is the quantum Lyapunov exponent, bounded by the Maldacena-Shenker-Stanford (MSS) bound: $\lambda_Q \le 2\pi k_B T / \hbar$.

### 3. The Mapping: FIM to OTOC
To bridge the macroscopic information deletion (Program 01) to the quantum scrambling mechanism, we must relate the Fisher Information metric to the OTOC. 

The Fisher Information about a parameter $\theta$ embedded in a quantum state $\rho_\theta$ is given by the Quantum Fisher Information (QFI), $\mathcal{F}(\rho_\theta)$.
Let the parameter $\theta$ be a small perturbation to the initial state generated by the operator $V(0)$. As time evolves to $t$, the state becomes $W(t) \rho_\theta W^\dagger(t)$.

Recent theorems in quantum information demonstrate that the decay of the QFI regarding the initial perturbation is directly proportional to the growth of the OTOC:
\[ \mathcal{F}(t) \approx \mathcal{F}(0) - \alpha C(t) \]
Substituting the exponential growth of the OTOC:
\[ \mathcal{F}(t) \approx \mathcal{F}(0) - \alpha \hbar^2 e^{\lambda_Q t} \]

### 4. The Quantum Scrambling Horizon
Just as the classical FIM approached zero at $T_c$, the Quantum Fisher Information hits zero at the **Scrambling Time** $t_*$. 
Setting $\mathcal{F}(t_*) = 0$:
\[ \mathcal{F}(0) = \alpha \hbar^2 e^{\lambda_Q t_*} \implies t_* = \frac{1}{\lambda_Q} \ln\left( \frac{\mathcal{F}(0)}{\alpha \hbar^2} \right) \]

### 5. Conclusion of the Mapping
The mathematical translation is exact. The "Information Horizon" $T_c$ where our Extended Kalman Smoother exploded in Phase 1 is the macroscopic shadow of the **Quantum Scrambling Time** $t_*$. 

At $T_c$, the local bits of information required to reconstruct the past have been maximally scrambled into global entanglement by the OTOC growth. The universe has completed its "Garbage Collection" of that local state, making it fundamentally unreconstructable for any local observer trapped in a causal diamond.
