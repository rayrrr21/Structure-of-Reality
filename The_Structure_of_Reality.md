### Part II: The Laws of the "Just-In-Time" Universe

### Part III: The Physics of the Observer (The Render Trigger)
A conscious mind is not required to collapse a wavefunction. The "Render" is a strictly thermodynamic and informational threshold:

### Part IV: Reality Hacking and the Limits of Agency

Because quantum entanglement and spacetime geometry are identical (ER=EPR), the observer's "choice" to measure a system breaks old entanglements and forms new ones.
