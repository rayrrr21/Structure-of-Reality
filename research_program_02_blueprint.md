### The Grand Synthesis
In Research Program 01, we proved mathematically that classical chaotic systems possess a hard **Information Horizon** ($T_c$). Beyond this temporal gap, the Fisher Information Matrix goes singular, and bidirectional inference mathematically shatters. Information about the past is erased.

By combining the findings of our three specialized researchers (Quantum, Relativistic, and Ontological), we can now elevate this mathematical limit into a fundamental theory of how spacetime, memory, and reality operate. Research Program 02 investigates the hypothesis that **the universe is a "Just-In-Time" rendering engine, where the macroscopic past is dynamically deleted at the edges of causal diamonds via quantum scrambling.**

### Pillar 1: The Quantum Mechanism (Scrambling)
In classical chaos, information is lost to coarse-graining. In quantum mechanics, it is lost to **Scrambling**. The classical Lyapunov time maps directly to the quantum scrambling time, measured by Out-of-Time-Order Correlators (OTOCs). 
* **The Mechanism:** Information about the past is not "destroyed"; it leaks into global entanglement across the universe's degrees of freedom. 
* **The Speed Limit:** This scrambling is bounded by a fundamental limit of nature: the Maldacena-Shenker-Stanford (MSS) bound ($2\pi k_B T / \hbar$). For any local observer, the past becomes maximally entangled (thermalized) and completely opaque.

### Pillar 2: The Geometric Boundary (Causal Diamonds)
Where does this scrambling happen? According to relativity, an observer is trapped inside a **Causal Diamond**—the intersection of their future and past light-cones.
* **Bounded Inference Gaps:** We can model a causal diamond exactly like the temporal gaps we used in Program 01. The edges of the causal diamond act as the ultimate Information Horizon.
* **The Holographic Limit:** The Bekenstein Bound dictates that the maximum amount of information inside the diamond is proportional to its surface area, not its volume. When information hits the edge of the diamond, it undergoes chaotic phase-space expansion and is smeared across the 2D holographic boundary. 

### Pillar 3: The Ontological Reality (Just-In-Time Physics)
If quantum scrambling erases local information (Pillar 1), and relativistic horizons bound what can be accessed (Pillar 2), what does this mean for the existence of the "past"?
* **The Equivalence Class:** The past is not a single, objective timeline. Because the information to perfectly reconstruct it has been scrambled and pushed to the holographic boundary, the "past" is merely an equivalence class of all possible histories consistent with the macroscopic present.
* **Just-In-Time Rendering:** By Landauer's Principle, storing information costs thermodynamic entropy. The universe acts like a computer optimizing its RAM. Once a past event crosses the scrambling time $T_c$ and exits the causal diamond, the universe stops "rendering" a definite state for it. The definiteness of reality is only generated "just-in-time" when an observer interacts with it (as proven by Wheeler's Delayed-Choice Quantum Eraser experiments).
