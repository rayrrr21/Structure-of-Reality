## Key Findings

### The Verdict: TSR is ~80% existing science

| TSR Component | Already Exists As |
|---|---|
| Core inference D(past)+D(future)→R̂(t₀) | **Smoothing** (Rauch-Tung-Striebel, 1965) |
| Quantum extension | **Past Quantum State** (Gammelmark et al., 2013) |
| Reconstruction Sufficiency | **Information Bottleneck** (Tishby, 1999) + **Rate-Distortion** |
| The phrase "TSR" | Already used in robotics, ML, quantum optics |

### What Might Be Novel (~20%)

1. **Cross-domain synthesis** — no single paper unifies all perspectives
2. **Information-geometric phase transitions** in chaotic reconstruction — untested hypothesis
3. **Finite-time reconstruction sufficiency bounds** — possibly distinct from observability

### Publication Readiness: C (Possibly publishable)

Requires experimental validation before any publication claim. Recommended first paper focuses on reconstruction phase transitions in chaotic systems, NOT on TSR as a "new theory."
