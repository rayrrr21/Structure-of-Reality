### Commandment I: The Landauer Toll (Observation is a Heat Engine)
* **The Mathematical Law:** Discarding $N-1$ counterfactual states to select 1 definite classical state dissipates minimum heat into the environment:
  $$\Delta Q \ge k_B T \ln(N)$$
* **The Narrative Canon (*Scanner Darkly* Translation):**
  * **The Retinal Ledger:** When a user doses on *Nadir*, they don't see a mystical vision; their visual cortex is forced to calculate uncollapsed probability branches. Discarding those branches produces real, measurable thermodynamic dissipation in cerebral tissue.
  * **The Landauer Fever:** Chronic users suffer literal thermal damage ($40^\circ\text{C}+$ brain fever, ruptured capillary beds in the sclera, and sweet ozone scent). Addicts are thermodynamic engines running at redline.
  * **The Crime Scene Receipt:** When an unrendered asset renders, it leaves behind an exact, unforgeable $1.43 \times 10^{-25}\text{ J}$ thermal haze that Evelyn tracks with cryogenic bolometers.

  $$S \le \frac{A}{4 G \hbar}$$
* **The Narrative Canon (*Three-Body Problem* Translation):**
